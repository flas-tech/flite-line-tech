01 For flight crews · Live

Wheelsdown

The crew-sourced layover guide.

Search by airport and see where crews actually eat, stay and unwind — plus candid FBO intel — from quick turns to multi-day layovers. Recommendations come from crews, and the community votes on what holds up.

  • Eat, Do, Stay and FBO intel organized around airport identifiers along your route.
  • Crew-vetted ratings with recent up/down votes, price and pace, and a “Go around” warning for places to skip.
  • Trip sheets and a route map to plan stops and share them with your crew.
  • Hotel program matching that surfaces lodging from the loyalty programs you use.
  • An FBO portal where FBOs can manage their listing and share QR trip cards with arriving crews.

02 For flight departments · Live

Standards Captain

See how your SOPs really perform on the line.

A structured, non-punitive line observation program for business aviation flight departments, built on your own FOM, GOM and SOPs — with modular safety tools and organized evidence for the standards you choose to follow.

  • Line observations with pre-brief, phase-of-flight ratings, threat and error coding, and debrief.
  • De-identified learning: completion records and observation data are kept separate, with small-group suppression in analysis.
  • Optional safety modules — FRAT, hazard reporting, inspections, tasks and training-event tracking.
  • Standards evidence reports mapping your records to requirements such as IS-BAO and SMS — evidence for auditors, not a certification.
  • Role-based access with MFA, audit logging and narrated training videos for every role.
